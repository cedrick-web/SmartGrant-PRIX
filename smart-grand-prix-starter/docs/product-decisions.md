# Smart Grand Prix: confirmed product decisions

| Decision | Confirmed choice |
|---|---|
| Interface language | English |
| Initial market | Rwanda |
| MVP scope | Combined business directory, intelligence hub, talent and opportunities |
| Blockchain | Professional and training credentials |
| Payments | Design and sandbox integration early |

## Product boundary

Smart Grand Prix is an independent platform. It must not imply endorsement, partnership, or official affiliation with Formula 1, FIA, Rwanda Government, RISE Motordrome, or any motorsport organization without an explicit agreement.

## MVP priorities

1. Business directory, profiles, services, verification workflow and enquiries.
2. Source-linked motorsport intelligence hub.
3. Jobs, internships, training and applications.
4. Admin moderation and role-based access control.
5. AI-assisted search and summaries after core search/content workflows exist.
6. Payment provider sandbox and architecture.
7. Credential issuance pilot only after an authorized institutional issuer is secured.

## Non-goals for initial MVP

- Building a blockchain network or issuing a token.
- Cryptocurrency exchange, wallet, escrow or stored-value functionality.
- Replacing official event timing or live-tracking platforms.
- Building custom machine-learning models before sufficient reliable data exists.
- East African expansion before Rwanda pilot validation.
